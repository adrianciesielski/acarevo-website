The Research Project

A model membrane based on a water-insoluble polymer.

The project investigated a model proton-conducting membrane based on poly(ethylene-co-vinyl acetate), or PEVA, for possible use in fuel-cell applications.

PEVA provided a water-insoluble polymer matrix. The work focused on preparing the polymer-based membrane, introducing properties relevant to proton conduction and characterizing the resulting material.

Compared with my later M.Sc. research, this was a narrower and less complex project. It provided an initial experimental basis for working with polymer membranes and proton-conducting materials.

Subsequent Research

Continued during the M.Sc. project.

The later M.Sc. research continued the work on proton-conducting polymer membranes using a different polymer system based on poly(vinyl alcohol).

Unlike PEVA, PVA is soluble in water. The M.Sc. project therefore introduced additional challenges related to polymer modification, membrane crosslinking, mechanical stability in aqueous environments and broader material and electrochemical characterization.
