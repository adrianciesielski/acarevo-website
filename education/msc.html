Proton-Conducting Polymer Membranes for PEM Fuel Cells

Research in polymer chemistry, proton-conducting materials and membrane development for electrochemical applications.

The Research Project

Development of proton-conducting membrane materials.

Proton exchange membrane fuel cells require polymer membranes capable of transporting protons while separating the reacting gases. The project investigated polymer-based membrane materials intended for use in PEM fuel-cell applications.

The work focused on the preparation and characterization of proton-conducting membranes based on polyvinyl alcohol and on understanding how the material formulation affected properties relevant to electrochemical operation.

Research Objectives

From polymer preparation to membrane evaluation.

01

Prepare polymer membranes

Develop membrane samples based on polyvinyl alcohol for proton-conducting applications.

02

Modify material properties

Investigate approaches for improving proton conductivity, stability and suitability for fuel-cell operation.

03

Characterize the material

Evaluate chemical, thermal and structural properties of the developed polymer membranes.

04

Assess application potential

Relate material properties to the requirements of proton exchange membrane fuel cells.
