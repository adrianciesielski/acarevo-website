01 Early-stage development

AI-Assisted Electrode R&D

Exploring how formulation data, manufacturing parameters, microscopy images and experimental results can be integrated to support electrode development for batteries, fuel cells and water electrolysis.

Current topics

Electrode formulation data Manufacturing parameters Microscopy image analysis Defect identification Design of Experiments AI-assisted experiment planning
Example Integrated electrode-development workflow

A development concept connecting formulation tables, process parameters and microscopy images to identify defects such as cracks and agglomerates and to support the selection of subsequent experiments. In the longer term, this workflow could provide the foundation for an AI agent supporting electrode R&D.
